Why another menu bar manager?

Older tools hide icons by stretching a giant invisible spacer across your menu bar and faking ⌘‑drags to shuffle things around. Recent versions of macOS broke that trick: icons disappear, the cursor gets hijacked, and the spacer itself gets kicked off the bar.

Ladelo doesn't move anyone else's icons. It asks macOS itself which apps may show in the menu bar and lets the system do the hiding and re‑laying out. When you pick an app from the ladder, it comes back right next to the helmet, so its menu opens in exactly the right place.

  • Hands off other apps. No fake drags, no stolen cursor.

  • Featherweight. About 20 MB of memory. The 3D firefighter only loads while he's on stage.
  • Private. No accounts, no analytics. Nothing about your Mac leaves your Mac.

Questions

Which Macs does it run on?

Ladelo is a universal app for Apple silicon and Intel and is built for the latest macOS. Hiding uses the system's own menu bar controls on new versions of macOS and a compatibility mode on older ones. The ladder works everywhere.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep the version you bought. Every 1.x update is free.

What permissions does it need?

Accessibility, so it can see which icons are on your menu bar and click them for you. That's it. Ladelo needs no network access to do its job and doesn't collect any data.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

The App Store sandbox doesn't allow apps to manage other apps' menu bar icons. Ladelo is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it without warnings.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Ladelo isn't for you, ask within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. See the refund policy.

Does it hide Wi‑Fi, battery or the clock?

No. Apple's own icons are left alone and always stay visible. Ladelo only tidies third‑party apps.
