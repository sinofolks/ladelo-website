30‑day money‑back guarantee

You can request a full refund within 30 days of your purchase, for any reason. No questions asked.

How to request a refund

Our orders are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. To request a refund, either:

  • visit paddle.net and look up your order with the email you used to buy, or
  • email us at hello@ladelo.app with your order number, and we'll arrange it with Paddle.

Refunds go back to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, it can take 5–10 business days to show up. Once refunded, your license key is deactivated.

After 30 days

After 30 days we can't guarantee a refund, but if something is broken, write to us and we'll do our best to make it right.
