Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Short version: Ladelo doesn't collect your data. This page explains the few cases where any information is handled at all. Ladelo is made by [SELLER LEGAL NAME] (“we”, “us”).

The app

Ladelo runs entirely on your Mac. It looks at which icons are in your menu bar and how often you click them so it can decide what to tuck away. That information stays on your Mac and is never sent to us or anyone else. Ladelo has no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

When you activate a license or redeem code, the app sends the key, an anonymous identifier for your Mac (a one-way hash of its hardware ID, so it can't be traced back to you) and your Mac's model name (such as “Mac15,3”) to our licensing service, so it can check the key and count how many Macs use it. About every two weeks the app checks in again with the same information, so refunds and revoked codes take effect. Nothing else is sent.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details to complete the order, under Paddle's privacy policy. We receive your email address, country and order details from Paddle so we can deliver your license and help you if something goes wrong. We never see your full card details.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security, for a short period.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email only to reply. We don't send marketing email unless you opt in.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing sinofolks@gmail.com. If you are in the EU, UK or another region with data protection laws, you have the rights those laws give you, including the right to complain to your local regulator.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
